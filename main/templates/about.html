Our Origin Story

Built for the Thrill of Live Experiences

Live events carry an electric energy that no virtual screen can replicate. Yet for years, attending concerts, matches, and summits was marred by server outages during flash drops, ticket scalping bots, misleading seating maps, and chaotic entry turnstiles.

We founded Kamnath Events with a straightforward mandate: eliminate friction between fans and live entertainment. By providing a clean, accessible platform with direct bookings, transparent pricing, and instant downloadable PDF passes, we ensure attendees and organizers enjoy an honest and reliable experience.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our tickets, security, and organizer partnerships.

Are tickets purchased on Kamnath Events 100% genuine and verified?
Yes, absolutely. Kamnath Events is the primary authorized ticketing platform for all listed events. Every confirmed booking issues an authentic pass with a unique Booking Reference ID (#BK-XX) and printable barcode that organizers verify at the event entrance. Counterfeiting or duplicate entries are strictly prevented.
How does the ticket booking process work?
Booking on Kamnath Events is fast and straightforward: browse upcoming events, select your preferred event, confirm your attendee details, and your official booking is recorded immediately with a downloadable PDF ticket pass.
How do I receive my ticket and do I need to print it?
Immediately after checkout, your digital ticket is available on your "My Bookings" page. You can download an official high-resolution PDF ticket pass anytime. You do NOT need to print it—simply show the QR code on your mobile phone at the gate scanner for rapid admission.
What happens if an event is postponed or cancelled?
If an event is rescheduled, your original ticket remains fully valid for the new date. If an event is cancelled by the promoter, automated 100% full refunds are initiated back to your original payment method within 5–7 business days without requiring complex claim forms.
How can event promoters and creators list on Kamnath Events?
Event promoters can reach our partnerships team directly via our Contact Support page. We onboard new events within 24 hours, assist with seating chart configuration, early-bird ticket launches, and provide on-ground gate check-in hardware.
